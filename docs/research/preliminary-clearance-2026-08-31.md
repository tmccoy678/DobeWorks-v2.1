# DobeWorks Preliminary Clearance Research

> **PROVISIONAL - NOT YET CLEARED**

## Status and purpose

This document records bounded, read-only preliminary research concerning the proposed `DobeWorks` name and the Marie/eclipsed-circle visual concept.

It is **not a legal opinion, trademark clearance, registrability opinion, or assurance that the name or design may be used or registered**. Only qualified trademark counsel, working from the final goods and services and the final mark, can provide that advice.

- Search date: **2026-08-31, America/Chicago**. Some official registry responses were timestamped **2026-09-01 UTC**.
- Intended field assumed for screening: software, methodological systems, research infrastructure, engineering, technical consulting, and related special projects.
- Research posture: read-only. No filing, registration, domain purchase, company-name reservation, publication, or contact with a rights holder occurred.

## Executive finding

The exact composite name produced no U.S. federal trademark knockout in the searches performed, and the principal exact domain names returned no registered RDAP object at the time checked.

That favorable exact-match result does **not** amount to clearance. The broader screen found a material same-field trade identity, **DOBE.dev**, already offering AI automation, custom software, websites, and business workflows. It also found relevant `DOBER`, `DOBERMAN`, and `DOBRMAN` identities in technology fields and a crowded dog-head-plus-circle/compass design field.

The preliminary screening signal is therefore:

- Exact composite spelling: no knockout found in the completed U.S. federal search.
- Dominant `DOBE` name element in the intended technology field: **material concern requiring counsel**.
- Marie plus eclipsed/broken circle or compass-like geometry: **crowded visual field requiring final-mark image review**.
- Overall status: **PROVISIONAL - NOT YET CLEARED**.

Internal design development may continue provisionally. Public launch, permanent signage, merchandise production, a trademark application, or other substantial public-facing expenditure should wait for counsel's clearance advice.

It also explains that goods and services can be related even when they are in different international classes. See [Federal trademark searching](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching).

This was not a comprehensive common-law search. Search engines can omit unindexed, local, new, private, or lightly used trade names.

## 4. Visual-concept screening

### Search method and field density

The USPTO design-code screen used these official categories:

- `03.01.07` - dog silhouettes
- `03.01.19` - heads of dogs, wolves, and foxes
- `26.01.05` - broken or dotted circles, including circles with more than two breaks
- `17.07.05` - directional compasses and compass points

The [USPTO Design Search Code Manual](https://tmdesigncodes.uspto.gov/alpha) also identifies `26.01.03` for incomplete circles and `26.01.04` for circles with two breaks or divided in the middle.

Completed searches returned:

| Design query | All-status records | Live records |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `DC:(030119 AND 260105)` - canine head + broken circle | 35 | 20 |
| `DC:(030119 AND 170705)` - canine head + compass | 14 | 8 |
| `DC:(030119 AND 260105 AND 170705)` - canine head + broken circle + compass | 1 | 1 |
| `DC:(030107 AND 170705)` - dog silhouette + compass | 11 | 7 |

These categories intentionally group dogs, wolves, and foxes, and many returned marks are visually remote. Counts demonstrate a crowded motif; they do not establish legal similarity.

No positive or negative WIPO result is claimed. See the [WIPO Global Brand Database description](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database) and [terms and conditions](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/terms_and_conditions).

## 6. Counsel-required next steps

Trademark counsel should:

1. Define the actual DobeWorks goods and services and intended jurisdictions. Likely areas for analysis include Classes 9, 35, 41, 42, and possibly 45 and merchandise classes.
2. Run comprehensive federal, state, common-law, and international searches for `DOBEWORKS`, `DOBE WORKS`, `DOBE`, `DOBER`, `DOBERMAN`, `DOBRMAN`, phonetic equivalents, spacing variants, misspellings, and similar commercial impressions.
3. Investigate DOBE.dev's owner, first-use evidence, geographic reach, customer base, service descriptions, registrations, and potential common-law rights.
4. Review Dober, Doberman Technologies, the Canadian DOBRMAN registration, and the other material records against DobeWorks' final goods and services.
5. Manually search WIPO, EUIPO eSearch, TMview, CIPO, and the national registers for every intended jurisdiction.
6. Run official image and design-code searches using the final manually constructed vector, not an early generated concept.
7. Compare the final design with the cited dog-head, broken-circle, compass, and red/black Doberman records.
8. Advise separately on the `DobeWorks` word mark and the Marie/eclipse design mark, including filing strategy, ownership, specimens, and goods/services drafting.
9. Issue a written clearance and registrability opinion before public adoption or filing.
