# DobeWorks

DobeWorks is Taylor's personal engineering system for methodological systems, software, research infrastructure, and special projects, together with the identity that represents that work. Selected source code and documentation are publicly shared in this repository. DobeWorks is not a public organization, hosted service, or commercial offering.

## Language

**DobeWorks**:
The complete, standalone name of Taylor's personal engineering system and its identity. Use it without a category or project suffix.
_Avoid_: Company, hosted service, qualified forms of the DobeWorks name

**DobeWorks Mark**:
The core, static DobeWorks symbol: Marie's left-facing silhouette crossing the divided Eclipse Ring. Refinement may perfect its anatomy and geometry without replacing, mirroring, or reorienting the concept.
_Avoid_: Mascot, generic Doberman icon

**Marie**:
The individual Doberman whose anatomy, character, and presence originate the DobeWorks Mark. Marie may be acknowledged within Taylor's private materials as its origin and standard of character, never reduced to a mascot.
_Avoid_: Mascot, fictional character

**Marie Silhouette**:
The specific contour derived from Marie's anatomy and presence, especially her long neck, refined wedge muzzle, shallow stop, and subtly asymmetric ears.
_Avoid_: Dog silhouette, breed clip art

**Eclipse Ring**:
The divided annulus surrounding the Marie Silhouette. It signifies eclipse observation, celestial navigation, precision, and engineering datum geometry.
_Avoid_: Target, gun sight

**DobeWorks Wordmark**:
The canonical name `DobeWorks` rendered as type alongside or independently from the DobeWorks Mark.
_Avoid_: Alternative capitalization

**DobeWorks Lockup**:
An approved arrangement of the DobeWorks Mark and DobeWorks Wordmark. The canonical family consists of symbol-only, horizontal, and stacked arrangements, with no required tagline or descriptor.
_Avoid_: Ad hoc composition, tagline lockup

**Motion Derivative**:
A presentation of the DobeWorks Mark through controlled motion, such as an eclipse-ring reveal, that never changes Marie's contour or the authoritative static geometry.
_Avoid_: Animated master, morphing mark

**Concept Study**:
A generated or exploratory raster used to evaluate a visual direction. A Concept Study may guide construction but is never traced or promoted as authoritative artwork.
_Avoid_: Master artwork, final logo

**Production Master**:
The authoritative, manually constructed vector form of a DobeWorks asset. A Production Master contains controlled geometry and no generated raster, embedded image, live font, or external dependency.
_Avoid_: Auto-trace, generated master

**Small-Size Master**:
A responsive Production Master with controlled optical adjustments for small rendering, such as stronger geometry or wider gaps, while preserving Marie's identity, contour, and orientation.
_Avoid_: Mechanically scaled master, redesigned icon

**Production File**:
A derivative prepared for a receiving vendor's actual color, profile, material, trapping, engraving, or embroidery requirements. A general digital export is not a Production File.
_Avoid_: Generic print-ready file

**Taylor-Controlled Environment**:
A device, local repository, application, dashboard, document, or personal physical artifact controlled by Taylor and not published or distributed as a DobeWorks offering.
_Avoid_: Public site, social account, shared asset library

**Private Use**:
Use solely by Taylor in a Taylor-Controlled Environment. Private Use does not authorize publication, marketing, external distribution, organizational adoption, or commercial activity.
_Avoid_: Public release, external offering

**Personal Release**:
A DobeWorks identity package approved for Taylor's Private Use after defined validation, independent review, and Taylor's explicit visual approval. Professional public-use clearance is not a gate for a Personal Release. No automated result or reviewer can substitute for Taylor's final human judgment.
_Avoid_: Publicly cleared identity, auto-approved output

**Public Release**:
Publication or external distribution of a named DobeWorks artifact. This source snapshot is public under its repository license. A release of the DobeWorks identity package, private sources, hosted service, or commercial offering remains a separate decision with its own clearance gates.
_Avoid_: Treating a source-code publication as approval for unrelated identity assets or services

**Private Source**:
An original Marie photograph used to establish identity and contour. Private Sources remain outside version control; the repository records their provenance and integrity without publishing the photographs.
_Avoid_: Committed source portrait
